Inside the church

  • Nave15.6 × 18.5 m

    15 rows, 20 seats per row, rows at 920 mm. Switch between 30 benches (5.4 m, 10 people each) and 300 padded folding chairs (540 mm each). Centre aisle 2.4 m, side aisles 1.2 m.

  • Floor2 options

    Switch in Settings: 600 × 1200 mm matt marble-look porcelain (anti-slip R10), or honed terrazzo with brass divider strips. Dark anti-slip strips on step edges.

  • Pulpit1.24 × 0.8 m top

    Oak, panelled front with corner columns and a brass SDA logo. Wide slanted desk with a book ledge, room for a laptop and an open Bible, and a gooseneck microphone.

  • Stage10.0 × 5.3 m

    Raised 600 mm, three steps with handrails both sides. Floor sockets, and a row of 12 upholstered auditorium seats (beam-mounted, tip-up seats with timber armrests), one shown folded down. Speakers hang from the roof to keep the floor clear.

  • Piano cornerright of stage

    Piano with bench, facing the pulpit. The walkway to the vestry door stays clear.

  • Children's cornerleft of stage

    18 low moulded-plastic chairs (300 mm seat) on a soft mat, facing the stage.

  • Projector screen4.0 × 2.25 m

    About 180 inches, high on the back wall. Projector hangs from the truss about 6 m away.

  • Power20+ points

    8 on the stage, twin sockets every 4 m on both side walls, piano socket, separate circuit for the sound desk.

  • Mothers' room4.7 × 3.7 m

    Glass window to the stage, sofa, nursing chairs, changing counter with sink.

  • Media & store4.7 × 3.7 m

    Sound and projection desk looking at the stage through glass, plus shelving.

  • Foyer & clerk's desk5.9 × 3.7 m

    Clerk's desk at the back, lower 3 m ceiling opening into the tall nave.

  • Vestry4.8 × 5.6 m

    Desk, three chairs, robe wardrobe, cabinet, sofa. Own outside door.

For the elderly and sick

  • Drop-off point: cars drive from the gate straight to the forecourt, at the same level as the paving.
  • 2 wide accessible parking bays nearest the entrance.
  • Ramp with handrails at the porch, and a second ramp at the right side door.
  • Step-free paved path from the forecourt and left side door all the way to the toilets.
  • Accessible toilet with grab rails, low basin and alarm cord; one low tap at the handwash station.
  • 2 marked wheelchair spaces at the front, beside the side exits.
  • Matt anti-slip floor, handrails on porch and stage steps, garden benches with armrests.

Light and security

  • Window setting: Standard windows are 1.4 m wide, Wide windows 2.4 m. Both 3.4 m tall, with 12 high clerestory windows above.
  • Lighting setting: "Daylight only" shows the hall lit just by the windows; "Lights on" adds the pendant lamps.
  • Raked timber ceiling up to about 9.6 m inside; tall slit windows in both gables.
  • Steel double doors: main entrance 2.4 × 3.0 m, two side exits 1.8 × 2.4 m. That gives 6 m of exit width for 300 people.
  • Steel window frames with burglar-proof bars.

Compound

  • Gate on the right, off the road. One paved entrance: 4.5 m driveway with a 1.5 m footway, running straight to the forecourt drop-off.
  • Parking on both sides of the entrance: 7 bays on one side, 6 on the other including 2 wide accessible bays.
  • Toilet block behind the church on the left, 12 m from the hall, with the back fence moved out about 14 m. Ladies 3 WCs and 2 basins, gents 2 WCs, 3 urinals and 2 basins, 1 accessible toilet. Quartz vanities, vessel basins, wide mirrors with LED strips.
  • 3-tap handwash outside (one low), and the 10,000 L rainwater tank right beside the toilets.
  • Caretaker's house behind the church on the right: living room, kitchen with dining, bedroom with wardrobe, bathroom with shower, front verandah.
  • Resting garden with pergola, bougainvillea and benches; jacaranda and nandi flame trees; existing tall trees kept.

Keeping the cost down

  • Machine-cut stone outside needs no plaster or paint.
  • A simple rectangle with one roof uses the least wall and roofing per seat.
  • Steel trusses and pre-painted iron sheets instead of a concrete roof.
  • Build in phases: hall and roof first, then finishes, then vestry and compound.
The front gable and gate sign use the official centered SDA logo in white on denim. This is a concept to help you visualise; an architect, a structural engineer and county approval are still needed before building.
